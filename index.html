The exhibition explores the momentum that carries an idea from curiosity to creation. It also considers how technology can help us understand and shape the world around us.

Science, technology, engineering, mathematics and art meet through hands-on experiments, creative work and collaborative projects. Together, they encourage critical thinking and imaginative problem-solving.

Migration

Student perspective | Tashika Rao, XI C

Migration:
movement at every scale

Migration does not begin only when animals travel from one place to another. It begins at conception and extends far beyond Earth.

Electrons move between energy states. Atoms diffuse, collide and rearrange. Cells travel through a developing body to help tissues and organs take shape. The same pattern continues through animal journeys, human history, planets, comets and galaxies.

  • Particles and atoms
  • Cells and tissues
  • Animals and people
  • Planets and galaxies

Movement is constant. It connects science, life and the journeys that shape our future.

Learning with purpose

School Citizenship Programme

Building the values and awareness that help students take an active, responsible role in their communities.

At The Indian School, our Citizenship Programme empowers students with essential life skills and values such as compassion, commitment, respect and resourcefulness. It goes beyond academics to nurture responsible and aware individuals.

Through classroom curriculum sessions, motivational workshops and activities such as Nukkad Natak, Shramdaan crafts and service visits to hospitals and senior homes, students gain a deeper understanding of their rights and social responsibilities.

  • Compassion
  • Commitment
  • Respect
  • Resourcefulness
